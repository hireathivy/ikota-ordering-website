Customer policy

To help us serve you smoothly, please take note of the following.

01

Orders & confirmation

Please send your order with the correct details. An order is not confirmed until you receive a reply from iKota on WhatsApp.

02

Special requests

Write any special instructions when ordering. Requests are subject to ingredient availability; please contact us about allergens before ordering.

03

Changes & cancellations

If you need to change or cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible. Once preparation has started, changes may not be possible.

Prices are shown in Namibian dollars (N$). Please confirm availability and collection arrangements with iKota when your order is acknowledged.
